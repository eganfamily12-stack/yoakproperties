At Yoak Properties, we are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal information throughout your tenancy journey.

Information We Collect

To provide you with exceptional property management services, we collect necessary personal information during the application and tenancy process. This ensures we can properly vet applicants, manage leases, and maintain a safe community.

  •  Application Data: Name, contact details, government-issued ID, employment history, and proof of income.
  •  Screening Information: Credit reports, background checks, and prior rental history obtained through authorized third-party services.
  •  Financial Data: Bank account or credit card details required for processing rent payments and security deposits securely.


How We Use Your Data

We process your information exclusively to fulfill our contractual obligations as your property manager and to improve our services.

Lease Management

Drafting lease agreements, managing renewals, and facilitating communication regarding your specific unit.

Maintenance & Support

Processing work orders, scheduling repairs with our trusted contractors, and providing emergency support.



Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for advertising. We disclose it only where doing so is necessary to run the tenancy:

Tenant screening

Applications are submitted and screened through Zillow, whose own privacy terms govern that process. Screening providers supply us with credit, background and rental history reports.

Property owners

We report on applications, tenancy status and payment history to the owner of the property you are applying for or renting.

Contractors and maintenance staff

Work orders are shared with our own crews and trusted contractors, including the contact and access details needed to carry out the repair.

Service providers

Our property management software provider, payment processors and professional advisers process data on our behalf under contract.

We may also disclose information where we are required to by law or by a court order, or in connection with the sale or transfer of a property.



Your Rights

You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, and ask us to correct it if it is wrong or out of date. Write to us using the details below and we will respond within a reasonable period.

If an application is denied on the basis of a screening report, federal law gives you the right to be told, to obtain a free copy of that report from the agency that produced it, and to dispute anything in it that is inaccurate. The adverse action notice you receive names the agency and explains how to reach them.

We keep applicant and tenant records only for as long as we need them for the tenancy, for our legitimate business purposes, and to meet legal and tax retention requirements.

Yoak Properties & Construction Co. is an Equal Housing Opportunity provider, and screening criteria are applied uniformly to every applicant.
